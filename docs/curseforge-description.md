# Guildprint

Guild recruiting for World of Warcraft: Forever. Write your ad once, post it in a click, and find players who don't have a guild yet.

## Features

**Advertise**
Post your guild ad to Trade, General, LookingForGroup or any other channel you've joined, with one click or keybind.

**Auto-prompt**
When you enter a capital city or a new zone, a small popup asks if you want to post your ad. It shows up at most once per cooldown and never in combat or instances.

**Find Players**
Search a level range with /who and get a list of players without a guild. If /who hits its 50-player limit, Guildprint walks the range one level per click so you see everyone. Tick the ones you want: Guildprint whispers them one every 3 seconds (at most 100 a day), or you invite them one click or keypress at a time. Anyone you whispered in the last 7 days is skipped, so nobody gets spammed.

**Replies and welcomes**
When someone you whispered writes back, a popup offers a one-click invite. Players who say "no thanks" or decline an invite are never contacted again. When someone you invited joins, Guildprint welcomes them in guild chat.

**Invite to Guild**
Right-click a player's name in chat and pick "Invite to Guild".

**Messages**
Keep up to 6 ads, a whisper and a welcome text. `{guild}` is replaced with your guild name, `{name}` with the player's name.

## Commands

- `/gp` or `/guildprint` opens the window
- `/gp post` posts your active ad
- `/gp next` sends the next queued invite

Both have key bindings under Options → Keybindings → AddOns.

Settings are also in Options → AddOns → Guildprint.

## Why does posting need a click?

Blizzard only lets addons post to channels, run /who and send guild invites from a click or keypress. That's why the auto-prompt asks instead of posting on its own, and invites go one per click. Whispers run by themselves once you start them.
