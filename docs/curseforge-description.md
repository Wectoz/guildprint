# Guildprint

Guild recruiting for World of Warcraft: Forever. Write your ad once, post it in a click, and find players who don't have a guild yet.

## Features

**Advertise**
Post your guild ad to Trade, General, LookingForGroup or any other channel you've joined, with one click or keybind.

**Auto-prompt**
When you enter a capital city or a new zone, a small popup asks if you want to post your ad. It shows up at most once per cooldown and never in combat or instances.

**Find Players**
Search a level range with /who and get a list of players without a guild. Tick the ones you want and Guildprint whispers or invites them, one every 3 seconds. Anyone you contacted in the last 7 days is skipped, so nobody gets spammed.

**Invite to Guild**
Right-click a player's name in chat and pick "Invite to Guild".

**Messages**
Keep up to 6 ads and a whisper text. `{guild}` is replaced with your guild name.

## Commands

- `/gp` or `/guildprint` opens the window
- `/gp post` posts your active ad (put it in a macro to bind it to a key)

Settings are also in Options → AddOns → Guildprint.

## Why does posting need a click?

Blizzard only lets addons post to channels and run /who from a click or keypress. That's why the auto-prompt asks instead of posting on its own. Whispers and invites run by themselves once you start them.
